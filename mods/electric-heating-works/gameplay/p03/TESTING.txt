PHOBOS ELECTRIC HEATING WORKS - P03 LOCAL TEST

Keep a disposable save. This revision is not published to Steam Workshop.
Name: Phobos' Electric Heating Works [P03 TEST]
This replaces the earlier local plant in the same item/object slot after exit.
The small construction probe is unchanged. Earlier packages remain archived.

Changes: an extra 30 cm ground clearance, sloped access at the entrances,
eight large heat outlets, double the heat and production electricity settings.
Staffing remains 30. Construction phases, cost selectors and cost coefficients
are retained; every costed node retains its bounding dimensions. Compare the
actual quantities because automatic cost calculations are performed by the game.

1. Before installation, record the TOTAL requirements for a NEW, unstarted C1
   construction site if convenient. Existing screenshots show remaining costs
   during construction, so they cannot establish the original total quantities.
   Save the disposable comparison game and fully exit. Tell Codex when closed.

2. After Codex confirms installation, start the game and place a NEW P03 site.
   Confirm the name has P03 TEST. Compare its starting material requirements
   against C1. No construction-cost rebalance is intended.

3. Check the pad on reasonably level terrain with small surface variations.
   The pad top is 33 cm above the reference plane (previously 3 cm).
   The entrances slope down to their previous outer heights. Check that roads
   and footpaths connect without a step, and that vehicles/workers use them.
   This does not make a 151 x 119 m site suitable for an unlevel hillside.

4. Check at least one partial construction stage, then completion. Previously
   completed parts should persist; scaffolding should disappear at 100%.
   Save and reload. C1 passed these checks, but P03 is a new geometry revision.

5. With sufficient electricity, workers and cold-weather demand, check the
   expected maximum production display: 420 GJ of heat and 60 MWh of production
   electricity per workday, versus the previous 210 GJ / 30 MWh. These expected
   display values are not yet verified; ancillary electricity may be additional.
   If stopped by its temperature setting, use cold conditions for the test.

6. Connect each of the EIGHT large outlets to a separate short large pipe and
   heat exchanger with demand. Check each individually, then all together.
   Avoid combining them through a shared bottleneck. Record plant production,
   power supply and downstream temperatures. Nominal 2,400 m3 pipe capacity
   versus an estimated 2,100 m3 requirement does not prove heat delivery.

7. Zoom slowly in/out and inspect flicker, shadows and detail changes. The main
   and first distance reference use identical geometry; the simpler second
   model is selected at 1,200 m. Save/reload the connected test layout.

Report the first failed step and a screenshot of the relevant panel/view.
