PHOBOS HEATING WORKS P02 - LOCAL TESTS ONLY

This package contains the construction probe and full P02 plant. Keep P01 installed
for comparison. Do not upload this item to Steam Workshop.

INSTALLATION HANDOFF
Save your current game, then fully close W&R and ModelViewer and tell Codex.
The checked installer will add local item 900000007 without replacing P01.
After installation, launch W&R and use a disposable test save. Do not convert an
existing P01 building or use your main city for these tests.

FIRST: CONSTRUCTION PROBE
1. Find 'Phobos Construction Probe [P02]' among mod monuments.
2. Place it on flat terrain and connect its road.
3. Use ordinary construction with materials and workers. Avoid instant completion.
   Start at normal speed so you can pause between phases.
4. During foundation, frame, panels and roof work, pause and deselect the object.
   Check that solid footings, blue-grey posts, tan walls and the roof appear before
   overall completion. Previously finished groups should remain visible.
5. Capture a partially built view with the phase/progress panel visible, then a
   deselected view. Report whether solid groups appear and remain through stages.
STOP HERE AND REPORT. If only a ghost and dirt piles appear, the construction
mechanism is still unresolved; do not spend time constructing the full plant yet.

SECOND: FULL PLANT CONSTRUCTION (AFTER PROBE REVIEW)
1. Find 'Phobos' Electric Heating Works [P02 TEST]' in mod heating/industry.
2. Place it on flat ground with enough space around the tank-side pipe outlets.
3. Before construction, record total workdays and material quantities for comparison
   with P01. These automatic costs are provisional, not an intended rebalance.
4. Connect the road and observe each phase at normal speed. Pause and deselect to
   inspect foundations, frame, panels, roofs, thermal and electrical equipment.
5. Confirm earlier groups remain, vehicles reach the site, and the completed model
   is intact at 100%. Report results before proceeding to visual acceptance.

THIRD: VISUAL AND DISTANCE REVIEW
1. Place a completed P01 nearby for comparison. Keep similar camera angles.
2. Check the hall window divisions, tank circles/railings, transformer shapes,
   four paired heat outlets, road clearance and overall switchyard arrangement.
3. Slowly zoom out and back several times. Initial distance changes are 600 and
   1200 metres; precise camera-distance UI is not required to spot a visible switch.
4. Orbit and repeat under day, sunset and night lighting. Look for sudden holes,
   silhouette changes, texture/shadow jumps or flickering on the ground.
5. Report what changes while moving; a single screenshot cannot prove no flicker.
   If possible, supply a short recording or before/after views and a description.

FOURTH: HEAT DELIVERY AND SAVE/RELOAD (AFTER VISUAL REVIEW)
1. Keep the same provisional 30 workers, heat and power settings. Supply workers,
   power, drinking water and required services. Use cold weather/known heat demand.
2. Test one large outlet at a time with comparable pipe lengths and an isolated
   consumer circuit. Start with the original outlet, then the three added outlets.
3. Record production, power availability, pipe/consumer temperatures and served
   demand after stabilising. Do not infer flow from a connection icon alone.
4. Connect all four to comparable independent demand circuits. Increase controlled
   demand and watch whether power, workers, production or distribution limits first.
5. Save under a new disposable-test name, reload and confirm all four connections,
   production and delivery remain correct. Report results before capacity claims.

ROLLBACK
P01 item 900000006 and its source files are preserved. Use the earlier disposable
save and P01 for comparison. Do not remove P02 while a save you want to retain still
depends on it. Any later package revision will use a fresh reviewed test handoff.
