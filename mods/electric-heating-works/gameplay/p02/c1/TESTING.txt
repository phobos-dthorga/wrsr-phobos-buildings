PHOBOS HEATING WORKS P02-C1 - CONSTRUCTION DIAGNOSTIC ONLY

WHY THIS TEST
The author reports multiple working stages on the small P02 probe, while the full
plant stays a ghost during construction. C1 selects the already exported simpler
plant mesh as its primary model: 25,562 triangles, with the same 38 named groups.
Construction assignments, coefficients, connections, production, materials and
textures are unchanged. This is a diagnostic, not the intended final appearance.

INSTALLATION HANDOFF
Save and fully close W&R and ModelViewer, then tell Codex when ready.
The current P02 item will be archived outside the active game folders before C1
replaces local item 900000007. Keep the successful small probe available unchanged.
No other mods or saves should be altered. Do not install over an open game.

AFTER INSTALLATION
1. Launch W&R and open a disposable test save.
2. Find "Phobos' Electric Heating Works [P02-C1 TEST]" in mod heating/industry.
   If it still says P02 TEST, stop and report that the old definition is loaded.
3. Place a NEW plant on flat terrain; do not continue an older construction site.
4. Record the total workdays and materials shown before construction. Automatic
   quantities may change with model bounds even though cost coefficients do not.
5. Connect the road and build normally at normal speed. Avoid instant completion.
6. Stay close enough to see individual hall windows. Pause in the wall or roof
   phase, deselect the plant and check whether earlier solid groups remain visible.
7. Send one partly built view with its phase/progress panel, and one deselected view.
   Report whether any solid foundations, frame or walls appear before completion.
8. Stop and report the result before further changes or heat/performance testing.

HOW TO INTERPRET IT
A pass would point towards a difference in the detailed mesh or its size. It would
not establish a particular triangle/vertex limit: C1 also changes small geometry.
A failure would show that substituting this simpler mesh alone did not resolve it.

ROLLBACK AND LIMITS
P02 and P01 sources and archived packages are preserved. C1 deliberately looks
simpler close up. It is not a visual-quality acceptance candidate or a Steam release.
No new model or texture sources were created; it reuses the pinned A06 LOD1 asset.
Restore the archived P02 package with the game closed when this diagnostic ends.
