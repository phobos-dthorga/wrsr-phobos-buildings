3Division: Workers & Resources: Soviet Republic and its native file formats.
The NMF was produced with the supplied 3Division exporter: Jan Kerkes (original),
Vladimir Baloga (Blender 5 update). The exporter is not distributed here.
DDS conversion used Microsoft DirectXTex/texconv; tool notices remain in the source
repository. These tools' authors are not the authors of the building artwork.

The render configuration references the base game's standard demolition debris
and effects, owned by 3Division. Those game assets are not included in the package.
