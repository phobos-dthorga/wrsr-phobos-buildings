PHOBOS' ELECTRIC HEATING WORKS - P01 LOCAL TEST

STOP POINT BEFORE INSTALLATION
Save any work, close Workers & Resources and close ModelViewer. Tell Codex they
are closed. Codex will install this prepared package and verify it. No publishing
or manual copying is required. Existing Workshop mods and normal saves are retained.

FIRST CHECK AFTER CODEX CONFIRMS INSTALLATION
1. Start W&R and use a new disposable test republic; keep your normal republic
   separate. Enable electricity and seasons. Use automatic construction for the
   first operational check so construction logistics do not hide a setup error.
2. Find "Phobos' Electric Heating Works [P01 TEST]" in the modded heating buildings.
3. Place ONE plant on a large level site, leaving room outside all sides for roads,
   heating pipes and HV lines. Do not mirror it during this first check.
4. Pause and send Codex a screenshot with the building selected and its connection
   markers visible. Report any loading/placement error. Stop at this point so the
   actual UI and connection positions can be reviewed before the power test.

NEXT GUIDED CHECKS (AFTER PLACEMENT IS CONFIRMED)
- Road and footpath enter through the rear gate behind the main hall. The vehicle
  station is on the rear service road. Confirm workers and service vehicles arrive.
- Two HV inputs are on the switchyard's outer/front boundary. Begin with ONE feed
  from a source with ample capacity, then compare two separately routed feeds only
  when instructed. Two drawn bays do not prove twice the delivered power.
- One large heating connection is at the twin header beside/between the tanks, at
  the site's right boundary in the plan. It represents one native heating link;
  the two visible pipes are the conceptual supply/return pair, not two outputs.
  Connect it to a short large-pipe route and a heat exchanger serving test demand.
- Water and sewage connectors are underground beside the rear entrance. They are
  domestic/service connections, not a claim of boiler make-up-water simulation.
- Record actual workers, productivity, electricity readings and heat readings after
  they settle. Record game version, enabled settings, ambient temperature and time.
- Compare cold/unpowered, powered with zero workers, and powered/staffed cases.
  Disconnect the supply only in the disposable test; allow time to distinguish
  residual stored warmth from continued heat production, then reconnect.
- Later: compare one versus two feeds, partial/full staffing, save/reload, winter
  demand and native construction stages. Keep a written result for each test.

PROVISIONAL SETTINGS
30 workers, native heat coefficient 350, per-second electricity coefficient 0.5.
These are test inputs, not advertised MW or GJ ratings. Final balance follows
measured behaviour. The two tanks have no separately verified storage simulation.

The full model has no LOD or emissive/night pass yet. The first test contains the
reviewed original Phobos art; the external A02 fence panels/lamps/barriers are not
included. This retains the same original appearance as the approved native test.

If the item is missing or a connection behaves incorrectly, report that first.
Do not upload this local item or use it in a valued save while it is unverified.
